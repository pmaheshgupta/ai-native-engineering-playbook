Your next 90 days

Most teams are at L1. Start the L2 build.

The first bricks are the cheapest with the highest return: a maintained CLAUDE.md in your top repos and AI review on every PR, with cycle time and change-failure rate measured so the next decision rests on data.

Action · owner · done when

Expect a dip before the gains. DORA's 2026 research finds productivity usually drops while teams learn new workflows and review load grows. Judge the week-12 numbers as a trend against your baseline, and look for change-failure rate holding steady, not just speed.

Fund the brakes as fast as the engine. Every level above L1 pays off only when verification and governance are built alongside adoption.

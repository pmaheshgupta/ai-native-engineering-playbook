Enterprise Agentic AI Platform

Persona requirements

As of 2 November 2026 · Mahesh Gupta

Each persona states 4–7 things they need from the platform or do on it, in their own voice. The number in brackets after each statement names the build module that meets it, so every need traces to something the platform team will build. Personas marked added in review were missing from the first version of the map.

Modules, in build order: 1 Gateway (LLM, MCP, A2A) · 2 Registry and identity · 3 Tracing, metrics and evals · 4 Runtime guardrails · 5 Cost attribution · 6 Context and data layer · 7 Developer portal · 8 Governance workflow · 9 No-code builder · 10 Value scorecard · 11 Prompt hub · 12 Data classification and DLP · 13 Knowledge and memory stores · 14 Sandbox, secrets and kill switch · 15 Environments and release pipeline · 16 Delivery and engineering analytics.

Strategy and governance

PersonaRequirements (module that meets each)
CEO

  1. I need one page each quarter showing value realised, adoption and top risks across all AI use cases. (10)

  2. I need to know which use cases to scale, fix or stop. (8, 10)

  3. I set the AI ambition and risk appetite that every other policy flows from. (8)

  4. I need assurance that no AI incident reaches the press before it reaches me. (4, 14)

  5. I need to see where we are ahead of or behind peers. (10)

Board / audit committee

  1. I need a quarterly AI risk report: incidents, regulatory exposure, control status. (8, 10)

  2. I approve the AI risk policy and want evidence it is enforced, not only written. (4, 8)

  3. I need to know which systems are high-risk under the EU AI Act or sector rules. (2, 8)

  4. I need directors briefed enough to challenge management on AI. (8)

CFO

  1. I need AI spend by business unit, use case, model and agent every month. (5)

  2. I need cost per business outcome, not cost per token. (5, 10)

  3. I approve business cases and want actuals tracked against them after go-live. (10)

  4. I need hard caps that stop runaway agent spend automatically. (1, 5)

  5. I need showback or chargeback to business units. (5)

  6. I need committed-spend contracts used fully across providers. (5)

CIO / CTO

  1. I need one inventory of every model, agent and MCP server, including third-party and shadow ones. (2)

  2. I set the approved models, clouds and patterns, and teams must not wire around them. (1, 7)

  3. I need to switch model providers without rewriting applications. (1)

  4. I need SLAs, disaster recovery and provider failover for critical agents. (1, 3)

  5. I need in-country processing options for regulated data. (1, 12)

  6. I need AI incidents and changes to flow through our ITSM process. (8, 14)

Chief AI / Data Officer

  1. I run use-case intake and need a funnel from idea to production with value at each stage. (8, 10)

  2. I need agents, prompts and connectors reused across teams, not rebuilt. (7, 9, 11)

  3. I need sources labelled and quality-checked before agents read them. (6, 12)

  4. I own the AI policy and need it enforced as runtime rules. (4, 8)

  5. I need adoption and AI-literacy metrics by team. (3, 10)

CISO

  1. I need every agent to have its own identity and least-privilege, short-lived access. (2)

  2. I need prompt-injection, data-loss and tool-misuse detection at runtime, feeding my SIEM. (4)

  3. I need shadow AI and unapproved agents discovered. (2)

  4. I need models, MCP servers and packages vetted before use. (14)

  5. I need to stop a compromised agent in seconds with a tested playbook. (14)

  6. I need red-team results for every high-risk agent before launch and each quarter. (3, 4)

CRO, General Counsel, DPO

  1. I need every use case risk-tiered and mapped to the EU AI Act, DPDP and sector rules before launch. (8)

  2. I need logs, approvals and eval results exportable as audit evidence on request. (3, 8)

  3. I need deletion requests honoured in sources, indexes and agent memory. (12, 13)

  4. I need vendor terms checked: no training on our data, retention limits, IP indemnity. (8)

  5. I need bias and fairness testing for systems that affect people. (3)

  6. I need customers told when they deal with AI, with a route to a human. (8)

CHRO

  1. I need adoption, time saved and skills gaps by team. (10)

  2. I run AI-literacy training and want completion tied to tool access. (2, 8)

  3. I set the employee AI-use policy and need it shown at the point of use. (9, 12)

  4. I need evidence of how agent work changes roles before any restructuring. (10)

  5. I need employee data in AI tools handled under HR privacy rules. (12)

Business unit head

  1. I bring use cases and need them live in weeks, not quarters. (7, 9)

  2. I need KPI impact measured against a baseline taken before launch. (10)

  3. I need agents inside the tools my team already uses. (1, 7)

  4. I need a named owner and support route when an agent misbehaves. (2, 14)

  5. I fund my share and need to see what I am paying for. (5)

Procurement (added in review)

  1. I need a standard AI vendor questionnaire on data use, retention, residency and training. (8)

  2. I need usage forecasts by provider to negotiate committed spend. (5)

  3. I need every AI vendor and SaaS agent registered before a purchase order. (2)

  4. I need exit terms and data-return clauses in every AI contract. (8)

Internal audit (added in review)

  1. I need read-only access to logs, approvals and change records. (3, 8, 15)

  2. I sample traces to test that policy was actually applied. (3, 4)

  3. I need evidence mapped to ISO 42001, NIST AI RMF and EU AI Act articles. (8)

  4. I need logs that cannot be altered after the fact. (3)

Responsible AI lead (added in review)

  1. I set fairness, transparency and harm criteria for each risk tier. (8)

  2. I review high-risk use cases before launch from a standard evidence pack. (3, 8)

  3. I need incidents that harm people escalated to me within a day. (14)

  4. I need model and agent cards published internally. (2)

Management and delivery (added in review)

PersonaRequirements (module that meets each)
VP Engineering

  1. I need cycle time, deployment frequency and change-failure rate per team, before and after AI adoption. (16)

  2. I need coding-agent spend per engineer shown next to the delivery gains it buys. (5, 16)

  3. I set standards for AI-generated code: review, tests and security scanning. (15)

  4. I need to see which teams are blocked by review load or quality problems. (16)

  5. I decide which coding agents we license and need usage data to renew or cut seats. (5, 7)

VP / Head of Data and AI

  1. I own the platform roadmap and need adoption, reuse and satisfaction by team. (3, 10)

  2. I need the platform team's cost and headcount set against the value it delivers. (5, 10)

  3. I need every use case through one intake so nothing is built twice. (8)

  4. I report platform health to the CIO and Chief AI Officer every month. (10)

Agentic AI platform product owner

  1. I run the platform backlog and need requests from every persona in one place. (8)

  2. I need internal NPS and time-to-first-agent for each new team. (3, 7)

  3. I publish the roadmap and release notes for platform users. (7)

  4. I need to know which modules are used and which are shelfware. (3)

  5. I decide build or buy for each module from cost and usage data. (5)

VP IT Operations

  1. I need AI incidents, problems and changes in our ITSM tool, not a separate system. (8, 14)

  2. I need the run cost of AI infrastructure alongside the rest of IT. (5)

  3. I need operational SLAs from the platform and from each model provider. (1, 3)

  4. I need on-call and support routes staffed for AI services. (14)

Delivery manager

  1. I need each AI use case's scope, milestones, budget and risks in one plan. (16)

  2. I need early warning when an AI project is slipping, before the status meeting. (16)

  3. I need environments, keys and data access provisioned in days so my team starts on time. (7, 12)

  4. I need eval results and approvals tracked as delivery gates. (3, 8)

  5. I report status and cost to the business sponsor weekly without building the report by hand. (5, 16)

  6. When we deliver for clients, I need per-client cost, SLAs and data separation. (1, 5, 12)

Programme manager / PMO

  1. I need a portfolio view of every AI project: stage, health, spend and benefits. (10, 16)

  2. I track dependencies between projects and shared platform modules. (16)

  3. I keep a RAID log with AI-specific risks: model changes, data access, regulation. (8, 16)

  4. I need benefits tracked after go-live against the business case. (10)

  5. I need the same stage gates applied to every AI project. (8)

Engineering manager

  1. I need my team's coding-agent adoption and where it helps or slows us. (16)

  2. I need review load and quality signals for AI-generated code. (15, 16)

  3. I need training paths for engineers new to agents. (7)

  4. I need per-team quotas so one experiment can't burn the month's budget. (1, 5)

Solution architect

  1. I design each use case from approved reference patterns. (7)

  2. I check which agents, tools and connectors already exist before designing new ones. (2)

  3. I need the data classification of every source my design touches. (12)

  4. I check latency, cost and residency requirements against the platform's limits. (1, 3)

  5. I record each A2A and MCP integration in the registry. (2)

Release and change manager

  1. I need prompt, model, agent and tool changes raised as change records. (8, 15)

  2. I need one release calendar for AI changes and application releases. (15)

  3. I need proof each change passed evals before I approve it. (3, 15)

  4. I need a rollback plan for every AI release. (11, 15)

Change and adoption lead

  1. I need active use by team, role and location. (10)

  2. I run a champions network and need to see where it is working. (10)

  3. I need training tied to the tools people actually use. (7)

  4. I need user feedback to reach the platform owner. (3)

Platform and operations

PersonaRequirements (module that meets each)
Enterprise architect

  1. I publish reference architectures for retrieval, agents and A2A, and need teams to start from them. (7)

  2. I need a live dependency map of agents, tools, models and data sources. (2)

  3. I need open standards (A2A, MCP, OpenTelemetry) so we can change vendors. (1, 3)

  4. I need standard MCP connectors for ERP, CRM and legacy systems. (6, 7)

Platform engineering lead

  1. I run the gateway, registry and runtime as shared services with published SLOs. (1, 2)

  2. I need a new team onboarded with keys, quotas and templates in one day. (7)

  3. I need tenant isolation so one team's load or data never reaches another. (1, 12)

  4. I manage every platform component as infrastructure as code. (15)

  5. I need capacity telemetry to plan GPU and provider quotas. (3, 5)

LLMOps / MLOps engineer

  1. I need dev, test and production environments for prompts, agents and models. (15)

  2. I gate every release on evals and roll it out as a canary. (3, 15)

  3. I need each trace linked to the prompt version that produced it. (3, 11)

  4. I need drift and quality alerts on live agents. (3)

  5. I run fine-tuning and eval pipelines on approved data only. (3, 6)

  6. I track model deprecation dates and test replacements in advance. (1)

Data engineer / steward

  1. I label sources before they are indexed. (12)

  2. I need retrieval to mirror source permissions for each user. (6)

  3. I re-embed changed sources and delete a person's data from every index on request. (13)

  4. I need lineage from any answer back to its source documents. (3, 6)

  5. I run quality checks on the data agents read. (6)

FinOps analyst

  1. I need every call tagged with owner, use case and cost centre. (1, 5)

  2. I set budgets and alerts per team and per agent. (5)

  3. I track cache hit rates and how much traffic routes to cheaper models. (1, 3)

  4. I need anomaly alerts within hours, not at month end. (5)

  5. I need coding-agent seats and usage in the same view as API spend. (5, 7)

Security operations

  1. I need AI alerts in the SIEM with agent ID, user and trace attached. (3, 4)

  2. I run red-team exercises and need a repeatable test harness. (3)

  3. I need playbooks for prompt injection, data leakage and agent takeover. (14)

  4. I need to quarantine one agent without taking the platform down. (2, 14)

SRE

  1. I need latency, error and availability SLOs for each critical agent. (3)

  2. I need automatic fallback when a provider fails or rate-limits us. (1)

  3. I need alerts before we hit tokens-per-minute or GPU limits. (3, 5)

  4. I need runbooks and on-call routes for AI incidents. (14)

Platform support (added in review)

  1. I take user tickets and need to open the trace behind a complaint. (3)

  2. I need a status page for every model, agent and connector. (3)

  3. I route a bad answer to the agent owner and QA in one step. (2, 3)

  4. I need onboarding guides and FAQs for builders and users. (7)

Builders

PersonaRequirements (module that meets each)
AI engineer / agent developer

  1. I need approved models, tools and data through one SDK and one endpoint. (1, 7)

  2. I start from templates with auth, logging and guardrails already wired in. (7)

  3. I need a sandbox with test data to try ideas safely. (7, 14)

  4. I need traces showing every step, tool call and A2A hop my agent made. (3)

  5. I publish my agent to the registry with an Agent Card and a named owner. (2)

  6. I need managed vector stores and agent memory I don't have to run myself. (13)

Application developer

  1. I use coding agents and need them approved, with single sign-on and audit logs. (7)

  2. I need AI-generated code scanned for security issues before merge. (15)

  3. I call AI features through a stable API with SDKs in my language. (1, 7)

  4. I need mock models for fast, free unit tests. (7)

  5. I need clear quota errors so my app degrades gracefully. (1)

Data scientist

  1. I need GPUs on demand and experiment tracking. (7)

  2. I fine-tune only on approved, labelled data. (6, 12)

  3. I register each model with a card before anyone can use it. (2)

  4. I share offline evaluation datasets with QA. (3)

QA / evaluation engineer

  1. I maintain golden datasets and adversarial cases for each agent. (3)

  2. I need evals in CI that block releases below threshold. (3, 15)

  3. I need a human review queue staffed by domain experts. (3)

  4. I turn every production failure into a new test case. (3)

  5. I test for bias, toxicity and data leakage, not just accuracy. (3, 4)

Product manager

  1. I define success metrics and acceptance criteria for each agent. (3, 10)

  2. I need usage, feedback and task-success dashboards. (3)

  3. I edit prompts and run A/B tests without a code release. (11)

  4. I need cost per task before I price a feature. (5)

Prompt / conversation designer (added in review)

  1. I write and version prompts in the hub, with review before production. (11)

  2. I reuse approved fragments for tone, safety and disclaimers. (11)

  3. I see how each prompt version scores in evals and in production. (3, 11)

  4. I need prompts tested in every language we serve, including Indian languages. (3)

Domain expert reviewer (added in review)

  1. I review flagged answers in a simple queue. (3)

  2. I mark correct answers so they become eval cases. (3)

  3. I see only data I am cleared to see. (12)

  4. I need my review time counted against quality targets. (10)

Consumers

PersonaRequirements (module that meets each)
Citizen developer

  1. I build agents from approved templates and connectors without writing code. (9)

  2. I need my agent reviewed and approved before colleagues can use it. (8, 9)

  3. I need clear limits on which data I am allowed to connect. (12)

  4. I see usage and feedback on the agents I published. (3)

Employee

  1. I need AI inside the email, chat and documents I already use. (1, 7)

  2. I need answers with citations so I can check them. (6)

  3. I need to know what I may and may not paste into AI. (12)

  4. I rate answers and report problems in one click. (3)

  5. I need my personal data respected and my conversations kept private. (12, 13)

Customer

  1. I need to know when I am talking to an AI. (8)

  2. I need a fast route to a human. (4, 8)

  3. I need answers about my own account, not generic ones. (6)

  4. I need my data used only for my request and deleted when I ask. (12, 13)

Partner

  1. I need a documented API or A2A endpoint to connect my agents. (1, 2)

  2. I need scoped credentials and clear usage limits. (1)

  3. I need contractual clarity on how my data is used. (8)

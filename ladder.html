Three rows decide everything: unit of change (a habit, a process, or a reorg?), returns shape (does the gain compound or go flat?) and team size fit (do the fixed costs amortize?). They tell a CTO whether a jump is worth it faster than any prose. Select a level to highlight its column.

PW = person-weeks, py = person-years. Costs are planning estimates. The fixed portion (~45 PW at L2) is the same whatever the team size.

Actions shift from buying, to building, to redesigning, to governing.L1 is procurement. L2 is a platform build. L3 is change management. L4 is governance. If an "AI-First plan" is a list of tools to buy, it is mislabeled.
